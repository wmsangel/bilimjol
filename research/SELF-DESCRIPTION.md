# Bilimjol

* URL: https://bilimjol.com
* Languages: ru, ky (Russian and Kyrgyz — both served across the whole product; no English/other locales on prod)
* One-liner: A bilingual (Russian/Kyrgyz) learning site for children in Kyrgyzstan, from preschool readiness through grade 11.
* What it is: Bilimjol is a web learning platform for kids aged roughly 5–17 (grades 0–11). Children practice short exercises by grade and subject, play educational mini-games, take self-check test-trainers, and earn stars to dress up a cartoon helper. It also includes practice for the ОРТ (Kyrgyzstan's national university-entrance test) and a career-orientation test for older students. The whole interface and content exist in both Russian and Kyrgyz.
* Who it's for: Primarily parents in Kyrgyzstan looking for structured, bilingual practice for their children — from checking a 5–6-year-old's school readiness, through daily practice in grades 1–11, up to ОРТ prep and career orientation for teens. Also usable by teachers as a supplement and by older students directly.
* What you can actually do (features):
   * Practice 1,200+ short exercises across logic, math, reading and "world/science" (окружающий мир), organized by grade (0/preschool–11) and by ~90 topics; multiple formats — single-choice, number entry, multi-select, ordering, matching — many with pictures.
   * Play 11 educational mini-games (memory, quick math, patterns, word-building, grouping, clock-reading, more/less, find-the-shadow, etc.), mapped to grade level.
   * Take 11 self-check test-trainers with randomized questions each run (multiplication table, addition/subtraction, division, squares, comparisons, a world quiz, parts of speech) plus an ОРТ practice set.
   * Earn stars by solving tasks and spend them to dress up a chosen cartoon helper (a dozen characters, six themed outfit sets); change the character anytime.
   * Take a RIASEC-based career-orientation test (aimed at grades 8–11): see your leading type and a 6-type chart for free; the full report (professions, study directions, subject plan) is premium.
   * Prepare for the ОРТ national exam via a dedicated landing and a pilot trainer (math + analogies/literacy).
   * Check a 5–6-year-old's school readiness with a short parent quiz.
   * Read 35 bilingual parenting/education articles; create an account to save progress, keep several children under one account, and sync across devices; optional read-aloud of task text via the browser's speech synthesis (helpful for pre-readers).
* Why it stands out: Fully bilingual Russian + Kyrgyz across the entire product (UI, tasks, games, articles) — Kyrgyz-language educational content is scarce, and that's the core differentiator for its market. It is gamified for self-directed practice (stars + collectible wardrobe + mascot) rather than drills, covers an unusually wide span in one place (preschool readiness → grades 1–11 → ОРТ prep → career orientation), and deliberately runs with no ads and no third-party ad tracking because it is a children's site.
* Access: A large part is free (many tasks, games, test-trainers, all articles, the school-readiness quiz, and taking the career test with its leading-type result). Guest mode works without an account. A premium subscription via Paddle ($1.99/month or $15.99/year) removes content locks across all classes/subjects and unlocks the full wardrobe + bonus games, the full career-orientation report, and the parent report. A free account is needed to save/sync progress; taking the career test currently requires sign-in.
* Privacy / data: Not an all-in-browser tool — it has a backend with optional accounts. When signed in, progress syncs to the server; guest progress stays in the browser until you register. Payments are handled by Paddle (merchant of record). Standard web analytics (GA4) are used; there is no third-party advertising.
* Best use cases (examples):
   * A parent sets 10–15 minutes a day of counting, letters and logic for a first-grader, with tasks read aloud for a child who can't read yet.
   * A Kyrgyz-speaking family practices math and reading in Kyrgyz, not only in Russian.
   * An 11th-grader prepares for the ОРТ exam and takes the career-orientation test to choose a direction.
   * A parent checks whether a 5–6-year-old is ready for first grade with the readiness quiz.
